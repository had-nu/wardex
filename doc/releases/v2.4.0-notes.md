# Wardex v2.4.0 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 2026-07-16
**Version:** 2.4.0
**Theme:** EU AI Act & Gleipnir-Anchored Provenance

---

## Highlights

### EU AI Act Framework
New `frameworks/eu_ai_act/ai_controls.yml` catalog + `pkg/catalog/eu_ai_act.yaml`
with **31 controls** covering all key articles: prohibited practices, high-risk
classification, risk management, data governance, transparency, human
oversight, accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity, provider and deployer
obligations, GPAI, post-market monitoring, and incident reporting.
`wardex assess controls.yaml --framework eu_ai_act` enables evaluation.

**Impact:** EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) compliance becomes a
first-class Wardex framework.

### Gleipnir-Anchored Chain Seal
Release artifacts (wardex binary, `wardex-config.yaml`, `README.md`) are sealed
with a chain hash anchored at block ~0 via Gleipnir-embedded.
`chain-seal-v2.4.0.json` carries the chain hash
(`8a27724a…7156e9a22b`) and per-artifact SHA-256 digests.

**Impact:** release provenance anchors to an unbiased third-party ledger, not a
self-signed claim.

### Isolated gRPC Driver
`pkg/provenance/grpc.go` moved behind a build tag to prevent protobuf init
panics in non-gRPC builds; `grpc_stub.go` carries the stub path.
